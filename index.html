Data Privacy Notice · Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

Information collected through this survey, including your name, email address, stakeholder group, age, affiliation, topic ratings, and any written responses, will be used only to administer the survey, analyze its results, and support Mapúa’s sustainability reporting and planning, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Only authorized Institute for Global Sustainability (IGS) personnel may access, evaluate, and reflect on the survey results. Please do not include student numbers or sensitive personal information in written responses.

How to answer

Rate all 19 material topics twice using the same 1–5 importance scale. For both ratings, importance means how much the topic matters in that context. Each topic includes a description and an example; your two ratings may differ.

Importance to stakeholders: How much does the topic matter to the needs, expectations, and well-being of people or groups connected to Mapúa, such as students, employees, alumni, partners, and communities? Consider your own stakeholder perspective and the people affected.

Importance to Mapúa University: How much does the topic matter to Mapúa’s mission, long-term goals, education and research, operations, resilience, and reputation? Rate its relevance to the University’s future based on your understanding.

These ratings measure perceived importance, rather than how well Mapúa currently performs. For example, a topic may be extremely important to stakeholders (5) and moderately important to Mapúa University (3).
